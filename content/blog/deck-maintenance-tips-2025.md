<p>A well-maintained deck can easily last 20+ years — and only needs consistent care throughout the seasons.<br>
Here’s a simple year-round maintenance guide to protect your outdoor investment and keep it looking new.</p>
<hr>
<h3><strong>🌷 Spring</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Wash away dirt, pollen, and winter grime</li>
<li>Inspect for loose boards or nails</li>
<li>Apply a light sealant if needed</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h3><strong>☀️ Summer</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Sweep regularly to prevent debris buildup</li>
<li>Move furniture occasionally to avoid fading spots</li>
<li>Re-stain or reseal if color looks dull</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h3><strong>🍂 Fall</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Clean gutters and sweep off leaves</li>
<li>Check for water pooling near the base of your deck</li>
<li>Tighten railings and inspect steps before winter</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h3><strong>❄️ Winter</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Avoid salt-based ice melt on wood</li>
<li>Clear wet leaves and debris so water can drain between boards</li>
<li>Keep the deck free of standing water or ice</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h3><strong>Final Tip</strong></h3>
<p>Regular maintenance means you’ll save money long-term and prevent costly structural repairs.<br>
A clean, sealed deck not only looks beautiful but also protects your home’s value.</p>
