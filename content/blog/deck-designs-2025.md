<p>Deck design in 2025 is all about <strong>creating outdoor spaces that feel like an extension of your home</strong>.</p>
<h3>Top Deck Trends:</h3>
<ol>
<li><strong>Multi-level layouts</strong> — split levels for dining and lounging</li>
<li><strong>Composite materials</strong> — modern, durable, and easy to maintain</li>
<li><strong>Built-in seating</strong> — benches and planters for smart use of space</li>
</ol>
