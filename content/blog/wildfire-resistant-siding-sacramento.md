## “Fire-resistant siding” is not a complete wall strategy

Home hardening works as a system. Cladding may resist ignition, yet embers can still enter through vents, collect at open joints or ignite combustible material beside the wall. The [CAL FIRE home-hardening guidance](https://www.fire.ca.gov/home-hardening) emphasizes roofs, vents, eaves, windows, decks and the immediate area around the home—not siding alone.

Before selecting a product, confirm whether the property lies in a mapped fire-hazard area and which current state and local requirements apply. Do not assume that a product appropriate for one Sacramento Metro address automatically satisfies another jurisdiction or assembly.

## Review the wall from grade to eave

### Cladding and joints

Ask for the tested or listed assembly, not only a broad material label. Fiber cement is noncombustible, but corners, trim, penetrations and the wall behind the panels still require correct installation. UC Agriculture and Natural Resources provides a useful overview of [siding and California Chapter 7A concepts](https://ucanr.edu/program/uc-anr-fire-network/siding).

### Vents and openings

Foundation, gable, soffit and attic vents can admit embers. CAL FIRE recommends approved flame- and ember-resistant vents or appropriate noncombustible corrosion-resistant mesh in specified applications, while warning that modifications can affect airflow. Ventilation and fire performance must be coordinated rather than improvised.

### The first five feet

Mulch, stored lumber, furniture and vegetation touching the wall can undermine a durable cladding choice. Keep the wall accessible for inspection and maintain drainage. Our [siding and sprinkler checklist](/blog/sacramento-siding-irrigation-clearance/) explains why the same transition also matters for moisture.

## Questions for the siding estimate

- What cladding, trim and soffit products are included?
- Which product listings and installation instructions apply?
- How will vents, penetrations and meter boxes be treated?
- What happens if removal exposes damaged sheathing?
- Are window, door and deck interfaces included?
- Who confirms local permit and fire-zone requirements?

## Frequently asked questions

### Is fiber cement fireproof?

Avoid treating any single component as a guarantee. Fiber cement is noncombustible, but actual performance depends on the tested wall assembly, joints, adjacent components, maintenance and exposure.

### Does new siding make a home wildfire-safe?

No single upgrade can eliminate wildfire risk. Siding replacement is an opportunity to coordinate cladding, vents, eaves, openings and the area next to the structure.
