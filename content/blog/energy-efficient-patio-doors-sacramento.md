## A patio door is a large window that also moves

Sliding glass doors combine a broad glazed area with rollers, locks, weatherstripping and a frequently used threshold. That makes product ratings and operating quality equally important. Before selecting a replacement, record the door's orientation, afternoon sun, shade, drafts, water history and whether the panel still locks and rolls correctly.

## Compare the complete product label

Use ratings for the exact door configuration. The National Fenestration Rating Council explains whole-product performance through values such as U-factor, Solar Heat Gain Coefficient and Visible Transmittance. ENERGY STAR also provides [climate-based guidance for windows, doors and skylights](https://www.energystar.gov/products/windows_doors_skylights).

### U-factor

Lower U-factor generally means less heat transfer through the complete assembly. Compare like-for-like sizes and operating styles.

### Solar Heat Gain Coefficient

SHGC describes how much solar heat enters. A west-facing Sacramento patio door may benefit from a different solar-control strategy than a well-shaded north-facing opening. Exterior shade can matter as much as the glass choice.

### Air leakage and operation

A label cannot compensate for a panel that does not meet its weatherstripping. Test the lock, interlock and rollers. Confirm replacement parts and adjustment procedures for the selected system.

## The sill and wall connection matter

The estimate should explain how the opening is measured, how the sill pan and flashing connect to the existing water-resistive barrier, and how interior and exterior finishes will be restored. A high-performing door in a poorly detailed opening can still leak air or water. Read the [sliding-door track and leak checklist](/blog/sacramento-sliding-patio-door-track-leaks/) before assuming the whole assembly must be replaced.

## Questions for the proposal

- Exact NFRC-rated configuration and glass package
- Frame material, panel count and screen
- Safety glazing and locking hardware
- Sill-pan, flashing and perimeter-sealing method
- Stucco, siding, trim and flooring repair
- Haul-away, permits and final adjustment

## Frequently asked questions

### Is triple-pane glass automatically best for Sacramento?

No. Compare whole-product ratings, solar exposure, weight, operation, cost and installation. The best package is specific to the opening.

### Can a sticky patio door waste energy?

It can indicate worn rollers, misalignment or damaged weatherstripping. Diagnose operation and air leakage before deciding between service and replacement.
