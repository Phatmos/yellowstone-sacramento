<p>Selecting the ideal siding depends on three key factors — <strong>climate, budget, and style</strong>.</p>
<h3>1. Vinyl Siding</h3>
<p>Affordable, versatile, and available in countless colors. Perfect for moderate climates.</p>
<h3>2. Fiber Cement Siding</h3>
<p>Resistant to fire, insects, and rot. Requires little maintenance and mimics real wood textures.</p>
<h3>3. Wood Siding</h3>
<p>Genuine look and feel, but needs regular sealing to protect against moisture.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>Tip: Always factor in long-term maintenance costs before making a final choice.</p>
</blockquote>
