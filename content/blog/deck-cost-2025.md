<p>But before you start sketching your dream design, you need to answer one big question:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>“How much does it cost to build a deck in 2025?”</p>

<hr>
<h3>2. Labor Costs</h3>
<p>Labor typically accounts for <strong>40–50% of your total deck cost</strong>.<br>
Professional builders charge between <strong>$20 and $45 per sq. ft.</strong>, depending on height, design, and framing complexity.</p>
<p>DIY might sound cheaper, but professional crews bring code compliance, safety, and warranties — which are worth every penny when you’re dealing with structural work.</p>
<hr>
<h3>3. Deck Size &#x26; Height</h3>
<p>The bigger and taller your deck, the more material and labor it requires.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Ground-level decks</strong> are the most affordable.</li>
<li><strong>Raised decks</strong> require additional posts, beams, and railings.</li>
<li><strong>Multi-level decks</strong> can double your costs but create amazing outdoor spaces.</li>
</ul>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Rule of thumb:</strong> Every extra foot of elevation adds about 10–15% to the total price.</p>
</blockquote>

<h2>Additional Costs to Keep in Mind</h2>
<p>Beyond materials and labor, there are <strong>secondary costs</strong> most homeowners overlook:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Permits:</strong> Typically $150–$600, depending on your city.</li>
<li><strong>Demolition or old deck removal:</strong> $500–$2,000.</li>
<li><strong>Site preparation:</strong> Uneven ground or soil work can add $500–$3,000.</li>
<li><strong>Finishing or sealing:</strong> $300–$1,000 for staining or water protection.</li>
<li><strong>Taxes:</strong> A new deck increases property value — expect a small bump in annual taxes.</li>
</ul>
<p>Always ask your contractor for a line-item estimate so you know exactly where your money’s going.</p>

<h2>Deck Construction Steps</h2>
<p>Building a deck isn’t just slapping boards together — it’s a full construction process. Here’s what goes into it:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Site Evaluation &#x26; Design</strong> – Measure slope, plan layout, and design renderings.</li>
<li><strong>Permits &#x26; Approvals</strong> – Local city review and homeowner association (if applicable).</li>
<li><strong>Framing Installation</strong> – Posts, beams, joists, and supports.</li>
<li><strong>Decking Installation</strong> – Boards, fasteners, and patterning.</li>
<li><strong>Railings, Stairs &#x26; Trim</strong> – Finishing details for safety and aesthetics.</li>
<li><strong>Finishing &#x26; Inspection</strong> – Staining, sealing, and final inspection for code compliance.</li>
</ol>

<ol>
