<p>Old, drafty windows can silently waste energy and make your home uncomfortable.</p>
<h3>Signs You Need New Windows</h3>
<ul>
<li>Condensation between glass panes</li>
<li>Drafts even when closed</li>
<li>Difficult operation or broken locks</li>
</ul>
<h3>Choosing the Right Upgrade</h3>
<ol>
<li><strong>Vinyl Windows</strong> – affordable, low maintenance</li>
<li><strong>Fiberglass Windows</strong> – durable, strong, and energy-efficient</li>
<li><strong>Wood-Clad Windows</strong> – premium aesthetics with insulated cores</li>
</ol>
<blockquote>
<p>For most homes, vinyl or fiberglass windows offer the best long-term value.</p>
</blockquote>
